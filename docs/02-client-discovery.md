# Client discovery (Mastercard)

## Our stakeholder: agentic commerce (Jossue + Victor's session)
A Mastercard representative focused on merchant services and the future of commerce (AI agents paying on people's behalf). Name/title not captured.

### Quotes and close paraphrases (use these verbatim in UI copy/pitch where relevant)
- **"NY to Florida flight, three layovers, money back: who's fault, who's responsible?"** ← the core example. Always say **three** layovers.
- **"How do I make sure this is a real agent making a real transaction?"** (named as the main pain point)
- "What if it books layovers when I don't want, unexpected things, hurts the merchants."
- "If you book a layover and they don't want it, it's got to get refunded, and that causes issues for the merchant."
- "How to make the agent make sure it does what it should, and quality."
- "Fraud transactions very bad." More sales and saving money are the goal.
- "I'd rather spend an hour doing it myself if it'll save money." (fragile trust in agents)
- Measuring success: **"always money, saving money."**
- Wants to ensure **competitors don't beat Mastercard to innovation**; "How do I get ready for the future of commerce?"
- Merchants: "They don't know how to fix it and how to make merchants implement." "They can help them think, but that's it." External help needed.
- **"We need something to encourage mass adoption, or something that itself brings forth mass adoption."**
- Compared to **tap-to-pay**: want seamless operations, but **"they can only tell them to use this new [method], they can't force them."**
- Mastercard's services sold to merchants carry its biggest margins. Airline / AA-style co-brand cards were the example, not a limit.
- When asked whether a process exists today: no clear answer captured (assume: existing chargeback process, case by case).

### Problem insight
Merchants accepting AI-agent payments experience refunds, disputes and fraud when an agent's purchase doesn't match what the customer intended, which costs them money and slows adoption of agent payments. We heard: "NY to Florida, three layovers, money back: who's fault, who's responsible?" A better outcome: every agent purchase is checked against the customer's approved requirements before payment, with clear evidence of who is responsible.

## Why we chose agent trust
Mastercard published its own open standard (Verifiable Intent) that explicitly leaves our client's question unanswered; real code we can build on; strong public data; low competition from other teams; fits the builders. See `14-decisions-log.md`.
